<objective>
Build the airport resolution layer: the state-to-region table, the metro ambiguity clusters, the
legacy-code alias table, the pure `resolve()` function, and the registry-backed allowlist that is
this project's single SSRF choke point.

Purpose: RESOLVE-01 through RESOLVE-04 and SEC-02. An analyst types "New England", "LA", "PANC",
"Santa Ana" or "PBI"; each must map to the right airport set, or say honestly that it is
ambiguous, or say honestly that it matched nothing — and no identifier that failed all of that
may ever reach an outbound URL in a later phase.

Output: `regions.ts`, `metroClusters.ts`, `aliases.ts`, `resolve.ts`, `allowlist.ts`, a shared
test fixture registry, and three test suites.
</objective>
